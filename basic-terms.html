Lesson 3: Basic Islamic Terms

Learning basic terms helps in understanding Islamic teachings.

  • Allah: The name of God in Islam
  • Muslim: One who submits to Allah
  • Salah: Prayer
  • Zakat: Almsgiving
  • Hajj: Pilgrimage
  • Halal: Permissible
  • Haram: Forbidden
  • Dua: Supplication or prayer
