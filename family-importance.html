Lesson 4: Importance of Family in Islam

Islam places great importance on family as the foundation of society. Parents, children, and relatives have rights and duties.

  • Respect and care for parents
  • Kindness to children and spouses
  • Maintaining family ties
  • Marriage as a sacred bond

The Prophet said: "Paradise lies at the feet of mothers."
