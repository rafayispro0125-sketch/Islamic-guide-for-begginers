Farz and Obligations in Islam

What are Farz (Obligatory Acts)?

Farz are duties that every Muslim must perform. Neglecting them is a sin.

Major Farz

  • Shahada: Declaration of faith
  • Salah: Five daily prayers
  • Zakat: Almsgiving
  • Sawm in Ramadan: Fasting
  • Hajj: Pilgrimage (for those who can)

Other Obligations

  • Treating parents kindly
  • Fulfilling promises
  • Avoiding harm to others
  • Seeking knowledge

Beyond Farz, there are Sunnah (recommended acts) and Wajib (necessary).
