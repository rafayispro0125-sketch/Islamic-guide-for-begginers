Keeping Iman Strong

How to Keep Your Faith Strong

Iman (faith) can fluctuate. Here are simple ways to strengthen it.

Tips

  • Pray regularly (Salah)
  • Read Quran daily
  • Remember Allah often (Dhikr)
  • Seek knowledge about Islam
  • Surround yourself with good company
  • Perform good deeds
  • Avoid sins and negative influences

Remember, Allah is always with those who strive to please Him.
