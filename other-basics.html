Other Basics of Islam

More Basics for Beginners

Here are additional fundamental concepts in Islam.

Halal and Haram

Halal: Permissible actions and foods. Haram: Forbidden. Muslims follow guidelines for pure living.

Islamic Calendar

Based on lunar months. Ramadan, Eid, and Hajj follow this calendar.

Charity and Kindness

Islam encourages giving to others, helping the poor, and being compassionate.

Modesty and Dress

Muslims dress modestly to show respect and piety.

Community and Brotherhood

All Muslims are part of the Ummah (community). Help and support each other.
