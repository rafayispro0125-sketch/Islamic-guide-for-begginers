Lesson 2: Life of Prophet Muhammad

Prophet Muhammad was born in Mecca in 570 CE. He was known for his honesty and was chosen by Allah as the final messenger.

At age 40, he received the first revelation in the cave of Hira. He faced persecution but spread Islam peacefully.

The Prophet's life shows how patience, mercy, and faith can change lives and communities.

Key Events

  • Migration to Medina (Hijra): This move helped build the first Muslim community and taught Muslims to trust Allah during hard times.
  • Establishing the first Muslim community: In Medina, Muslims learned to live together with respect, justice, and care for neighbors.
  • Conquests and treaties: The Prophet worked for peace when possible and defended the community when necessary.
  • His character: Kind, just, and merciful. He treated people fairly and helped those in need.

Learning about the Prophet's character helps beginners understand why Muslims follow his example in daily life.
