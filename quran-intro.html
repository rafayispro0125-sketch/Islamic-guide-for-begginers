Lesson 1: Introduction to the Quran

The Quran is the holy book of Islam, revealed to Prophet Muhammad over 23 years. It is the word of Allah, preserved in its original Arabic.

It consists of 114 chapters (Surahs) and contains guidance for all aspects of life.

Every chapter teaches something important about faith, family, justice, and kindness.

  • The Quran is the final revelation
  • It teaches monotheism, morality, and laws
  • Muslims recite it in prayers and daily life

How to Begin

Start with short verses from the opening chapter, Al-Fatiha, and then read simple clear passages from later chapters. Many learners also use a translation in their own language to understand the meaning.

Reading even a small amount each day helps build familiarity, comfort, and respect for the message the Quran carries.
