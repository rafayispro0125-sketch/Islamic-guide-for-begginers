What is Islam?

Understanding Islam

Islam is a monotheistic religion that teaches the existence of one God, Allah, and the prophethood of Muhammad as the final messenger. The word "Islam" means "submission to the will of Allah."

Muslims believe in the Quran as the holy book revealed to Prophet Muhammad, and follow the Sunnah (teachings and actions of the Prophet).

Islam emphasizes peace, compassion, justice, and worship of Allah alone.

It also teaches respect for all people, kindness to neighbors, and honesty in every part of life.

Key Beliefs

  • Tawhid: Oneness of God
  • Prophethood: Belief in prophets like Adam, Noah, Abraham, Moses, Jesus, and Muhammad
  • Afterlife: Belief in heaven, hell, and judgment day
  • Quran and Sunnah: Divine guidance

When people understand these core beliefs, they can see how Islam forms a complete way of life based on mercy, justice, and personal responsibility.
